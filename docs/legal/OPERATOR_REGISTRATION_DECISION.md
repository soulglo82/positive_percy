# Positive Percy — Operator identity and company registration decision (12 Nov 2026)

**Current confirmed position:** Positive Percy is not yet an incorporated company or registered business, and no dedicated business phone number exists. Do not describe it as "Positive Percy Ltd", "Pte Ltd", or state a company registration number until an incorporation actually occurs.

## Controller for pre-launch usage

Data protection responsibility can sit with the individual operating the service; UK ICO guidance recognises natural persons/sole traders as controllers. Being unincorporated does not exempt processing children's personal data from applicable law. If Percy is accessible to customers beyond personal/household use, identify the operator and provide a monitored privacy-contact channel before publishing policies.

**Still required before finalising draft policy:** the individual's legal name (for the controller notice), a monitored privacy-contact email or suitable contact address, business/service jurisdiction, actual processor/hosting regions, basis for processing and response channel for data requests. A business telephone number is not essential to UK incorporation under the published registered-office/email rules; ensure the chosen contact route meets the privacy rules that actually apply.

## UK Ltd option (remote director)

- A director does **not** need to live in the UK.
- Must have a real, appropriate UK registered-office postal address and registered email; a professional mail service is possible.
- Companies House mandatory director/PSC identity verification.
- Online incorporation fee **£100** from 1 Feb 2026; online annual confirmation statement **£50** plus accounts, tax and provider costs.
- UK incorporation does not automatically prevent Singapore tax/business obligations where the founder manages the company from Singapore. HMRC incorporates the company into UK tax residence (subject to treaty exceptions); IRAS examines effective management/control in Singapore.

## Singapore Pte Ltd option

- ACRA statutory fees: **S$15 name + S$300 registration** (excluding corporate-service-provider/secretary and address costs).
- At least one ordinarily Singapore-resident director; secretary appointed within six months.
- Foreigners generally need a registered corporate service provider. Personal immigration/work-pass conditions may affect whether a person may actively run a business in Singapore.
- Operating a business regularly for profit under a brand name may require ACRA registration even if incorporated elsewhere.

Sources:
- https://www.acra.gov.sg/manage/companies/service-transaction-fees/
- https://www.acra.gov.sg/register/business/requirements-eligibility/
- https://www.acra.gov.sg/register/business/registering-different-business-structures/local-company/appointing-company-directors-other-key-officers/

## Recommendation

Do **not** incorporate just to unblock a software release. Keep the policy in DRAFT, document the actual individual operator and monitored privacy contact when supplied, and arrange a short qualified cross-border tax/business compliance assessment before commercial launch or charging parents. Choose UK vs Singapore based on actual management, intended customer base and immigration/work rights, not just a low registration fee.

Do not publish legal text without review.
