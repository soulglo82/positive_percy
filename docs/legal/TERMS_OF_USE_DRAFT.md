## What Positive Percy does

Positive Percy helps grown-ups recognise positive behaviour by awarding points, celebrating achievements and organising family rewards. Points are a family-game record; they are not money, a wallet balance, an asset, a stored-value instrument or a guarantee that a promised real-world reward will be fulfilled.

## Who may use it

A responsible adult creates and manages the family, chooses what information to enter and supervises any child who views the app. The service is currently parent-operated and does not create separately permissioned child accounts.

## Family access code

A family code grants access to family information and controls. Only share it with trusted people authorised to manage your family. Until separate adult accounts and permission levels are implemented, anyone with the code can make family-wide changes. Keep a secure copy before signing out.

## Reward choices and point corrections

Grown-ups decide which positive behaviours to reward and the cost of each reward. Percy does not automatically enforce household rules or deliver physical or digital rewards. Adults are responsible for providing, declining or changing a promised household reward, communicating with children and ensuring a safe, fair experience.

Percy does not allow new punitive point deductions. Mistaken point awards can be corrected through Activity within the same family week, subject to available unspent/unreserved points. Historical adjustments may remain visible.

## Appropriate content

Only upload pictures or enter information you are entitled to use; avoid private sensitive details, harmful content or other people's information without proper permission. Do not attempt to access a different family's data, interfere with the service or misuse the family-code mechanism.

## Availability, backups and downloads

Data may be temporarily unavailable due to maintenance or interruptions. Use the Settings export if you want your own copy. The Family Report PDF is a summary; technical JSON contains retained historic data. An export is not currently a one-click restore file.
