Locked design language

Parent-facing, positive, calm, and playful without feeling like a game.

  • Fox 1A: slender seated mascot, one yellow star
  • Headings: Nunito 700/800
  • Interface: Poppins 400–600
  • Icons: official Phosphor line geometry
  • Primary actions: purple with white text
  • Rewards: Phosphor fallback until Streamline picker licence confirmed
Brand-art gate: the 1A fox shown here is a temporary hand-drawn silhouette matching the approved direction, not the certified master. The actual vector and outlined wordmark must replace it before production. The mockup's icons are inline SVGs from the MIT-licensed Phosphor source; reward tiles use Phosphor fallback, not Streamline assets.
