## Limitations and approval gates

1. **Semantic gaps:** Streamline Freehand’s 1,000-icon free subset lacks exact gift and generic profile glyphs. The menu comparison uses genuine shopping-bags for Rewards and a person-in-message glyph for Progress. These are weaker semantic matches, not approved replacements. Cyber Home similarly uses a complex home-scene icon. **Do not approve any family purely from the visuals without checking availability of correct semantics**.
2. **Sizes:** icon paths are unmodified and nested at 34 px for menu and 83 px for rewards; check real 20–24 px navigation legibility before sign-off.
3. **Licence:** Streamline’s public [repository README](https://github.com/webalys-hq/streamline-vectors#open-source-license) states CC BY 4.0 and attribution/link obligations. Since Percy offers a parent-selectable icon catalogue, final product licensing and attribution treatment still require validation before shipping. This preview is comparison/review only.
4. **Brand:** the Fox 1A concept and final star are excluded to isolate icon differences; there is **no substituted AI mascot or generated reward graphic**. Branding remains the approved Fox 1A, Nunito/Poppins and purple/orange/cream direction.
5. **Product:** all labels and four reward examples are illustrative. No changes to parent-only permissions, goal ledger, points or reward economics.
6. **Prior proposals:** the images previously described as Freehand / Cyber equivalents were AI generations and are superseded by these sourced comparisons.

**Asset registry schema for production:** `asset_id`, `library`, `family`, `variant`, `local_file_path`, `license_reference`, `semantic_name`, optional `legacy_emoji`. Copy upstream SVG files into a reviewed local registry only after licensing and design selection.
