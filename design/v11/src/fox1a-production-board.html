What the illustrator delivers for each pose

Sign-off check for every pose

  • Side by side with the seated master at the same height.
  • Face close-up: pointed muzzle, closed smiling eye, ear shape.
  • Tail close-up: direction, curl and the white swoop marking.
  • Same orange, same white areas, one yellow star. No extra sparkles in the artwork.
  • Not mirrored. Reads at 48px.

Where Percy appears

Each placement asks for a stable key. Until the artwork exists, every key shows the seated master, so screens can be built now and the art swapped in later without touching them.

Motion storyboards

Seven motions. Three can be shown today because they move the whole figure or the star, which is already its own shape. The other four need a limb to move, so they need layered artwork first. Short, once, then still; only the idle loop repeats.

Decisions for the owner

  1. Source. Can you supply the designer's vector file or the full-resolution refinement board? If not, approve commissioning a clean, layered redraw of the seated master.
  2. Colours. Is the star #FFA600 (as traced) or sunshine #FFC938? Are the inner areas white or cream?
  3. Poses. Confirm the five approved concepts go to an illustrator, and choose from the six proposals (recommended: waving, celebrating, pointing).
  4. Motion. Approve the three motions shown here as the interim set, built from the unaltered master.
