Typography

Nunito 700/800 for headings and numbers, Poppins 400/500/600 for everything else (both locked). Nine roles replace the one-off sizes. Sizes are in rem, so a larger browser text setting scales every role together. The smallest text a parent has to read to act is 14px; 12px is used only for tab labels and overlines.

Rule: a label never shrinks to fit. When four tiles do not fit, the grid drops to two columns; when a name is long, it wraps.

Colour

The brand palette and V1 semantic colours are unchanged. Five soft tints are new and are used only for scene backgrounds and tile fills, never for text. Orange fails contrast as text on white (2.87:1), so it stays a graphic colour; --pv-orange-text is the text-safe orange.

Spacing, radius, elevation

An 8px cadence with 4px substeps. One content edge per screen: 16px on phones, 32px from 834px. Three radii, three shadows.

Child photos

The photo is the child's identity on screen, so it is large: 72px for the selected child on Home (was 32 to 48px in V1), 52px for the others, 104px on the Children profile, and larger again from 834px. A missing or failed photo shows the initial at exactly the same size.

No photographs ship in this review. The repository holds no child photos, and none were invented or downloaded. The "photo" specimens below are labelled crop test cards that prove the cropping rule. To see real photos in the components, choose images from your own device: they stay in this browser tab and are not uploaded.

Components

ChildSelector

Horizontal and scrollable, so six children never shrink to fit. The selected child is larger, ringed in their own colour and underlined. Names are always shown; a long name wraps to two lines here and is shown in full in the page heading. With one child the selector is not rendered.

QuickPointTile

Two columns on phones, four from 834px. Label 15px, never smaller. The whole tile is the button (at least 110px tall).

TodayGoalRow

A 44px check target, a 16px title that wraps, and Undo once done. The empty state says what to do next.

PercyProgressRings

Three metrics only: today's goals done / scheduled, points earned / weekly target, and one named goal saved / cost. The arc stops at full; the number beside it is never clamped. A zero denominator draws a neutral dashed ring with no percentage. Available points is a balance and has no ring.

BiggerGoalDestination

Where the Children journey ends: the named goal, what is saved, what is left, and the way into Savings.

AppNavigation

The approved five tabs, unchanged. A bottom bar up to 1099px; a side rail with 15px labels from 1100px. Tab labels are the one interactive use of 12px text.

PercyHeroSlot

Screens ask for a stable key (fox1a.seated, fox1a.walking and so on). Until source-faithful pose artwork exists, every key resolves to the seated Fox 1A master, unaltered. Three sizes: 48, 96 and hero (144px on phones, 208px from 834px).

Motion

Four durations and two easings. Percy responds once to an action and then stops; only the idle star twinkle loops. With "reduce motion" on, nothing animates and every state is still shown.

Decisions for the owner

  1. Type scale. Approve the nine roles as shown, or name the role to change.
  2. Tab labels at 12px. Five tabs at 320px leave 64px each. 12px is the only size where "Children" and "Settings" fit without clipping. Approve, or switch to icon-only tabs under 360px.
  3. Photo sizes. 72 / 52 / 104px on phones; 88 / 64 / 136px from 834px.
  4. Scene colours. Five new soft tints (warm sky, peach, lilac mist, two meadow greens) used only as backgrounds.
  5. Scene drawings. The sun, hill, clouds and flag are simple shapes drawn in code as placeholders for the look. They are not illustrator artwork.
