## How to judge

Check recognition by a parent at **20–24 px** with a label, and against Percy's cream background. The `Duotone` candidates below are uncoloured official geometry; selected items can be tinted using approved tokens in React. **A badge, number or star must not substitute for the separately owned Percy points-star.**

## Restrictions

- No icon is selected by its position in the table.
- The [alternative Streamline menu families](../previews/shipready-v3-menu-real-4-families.svg) are genuine original SVGs and are style comparison candidates, *not approved semantic mappings*. That board intentionally illustrates some weak substitutions.
- No mixing icon libraries within the same five-tab row until an explicit visual choice; baseline is MIT Phosphor per the fidelity brief.
- The dedicated brand star for points, Fox 1A mascot and its wordmark remain owned assets, not replaceable by icon-library glyphs.
- React integration approval requires exact imports (capitalised component names), actual regular/fill weights, responsive captures at **390 / 834 / 1280 px**.
