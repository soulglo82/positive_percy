Contrast and usage

Primary actions

Create a Goal

White lettering on Percy purple. Avoid white text on vivid orange for normal text.

Text and backgrounds

Ink on white or cream for body. Use purple for headings and links. Reserve yellow and orange for highlights, panels, non-essential ornaments and selected state decorations.

Responsive design tokens

Tap target: at least 44 × 44 CSS px, with 24 × 24 px goal icon. Reward picker: 3 columns when container width is below 520px; 5 at 520–759px; 6 at 760px and above.

The picker is container-responsive, not browser-width responsive. Verify real sizes and selected states in the React preview.

Fox 1A is a fixed brand choice

Approved design direction

Sleek, slender seated Fox 1A with one yellow star, paired with a custom purple wordmark. The complete Fox 1A SVG and PNG logo set is included under 05_Logos/Fox_1A_Complete_Logo_Kit/, source-traced from the approved 1A board. These are editable vector outlines, not the original illustrator control points.

Do not: use the former fox-with-foliage logo, an AI-redrawn substitute fox, the 1B variant, or a different fox for social and documents.

Dedicated points currency star

The numeric points balance uses its own approved brand star concept. The old Lucide star component is a legacy implementation, not the verified production master. Decorative Streamline star stickers are unrelated to the points-currency glyph.

Logo export formats

These formats are now exported in the Fox 1A Complete Logo Kit: vector mascot, wordmark, horizontal and stacked logos, favicons, PWA icons, iOS and Android artwork, social avatars and print-friendly logos. Original designer control-point masters were not supplied. See 05_Logos/LOGO_SET_EXPORT_SPEC.md.

Font source: Google Fonts Nunito and Poppins, under SIL OFL. No font binaries distributed here.

Website and documentation

Marketing website

Use Fox 1A + wordmark on arrival pages, purple primary CTA, Nunito headlines, Poppins body, warm cream backgrounds and limited cheerful art. Keep illustrations coherent with the app, not a second visual style.

PDFs and family reports

Use the horizontal wordmark, same palette and type hierarchy. Keep figures and point balances legible in print. Include approved points star only once its master has been verified.

Illustrations are pinned to exact source IDs. This guide never substitutes alternative art for selected assets.
